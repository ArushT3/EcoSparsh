Get Involved

Every senior deserves dignity. Every caregiver deserves support.

EcoSparsh's work is powered by volunteers, donors, and partners who believe in building a more graceful way to age. Here's how you can help.

Donate

Your contribution directly funds health & social care centre operations, caregiver support programs, training, and community outreach — the proof-of-concept work EcoSparsh is running today in partnership with Rotary Bangalore Oasis and its community partners.

To make a donation, please reach out to us directly using the contact details below and we'll guide you through the process.

Volunteer

EcoSparsh welcomes volunteers of every background:

  • Volunteers — young students and others willing to serve seniors through our camps, awareness programs, and caregiver circles.
  • Professionals — people pursuing a career in nursing or related fields, looking to gain hands-on experience in elder care.
