Projects

Our flagship initiatives

1

SeniorCare Community Camp26 January 2025Completed

Organized by EcoSparsh and the Zemyna Foundation, this Senior Care Camp & Fair brought together a holistic blend of health screenings, wellness activities, financial guidance, accessibility support, and engaging experiences tailored for seniors. Senior residents of the community had the opportunity to meet and connect with their neighbors over wholesome food, soulful live music, and brain-stimulation activities. Attendees explored longevity insights inspired by ancient wisdom and visited thoughtfully curated stalls promoting positive ageing.

2

Awareness ProgramOngoing

A project-based awareness program that engages and educates youth on the importance of senior care, with a special focus on mental well-being and the role of caregivers. Through interactive experiences, discussions, and community involvement, it builds empathy and a sense of responsibility among younger generations.

Delivered with:

  • Rotaract Club of Presidency University
  • People's Trust
  • Sunandi Wellbeing
  • Maarga
  • MindCare
  • Nagarjuna College
  • ...and others, in person
3

Fund Raising Event: Junior Charity Golf16 May 2025

EcoSparsh partnered with Citadel to organize "Junior Charity Golf." Alongside this, EcoSparsh is collaborating with Sunandi Wellbeing to launch a dedicated program supported by an initial CSR grant of ₹1 lakh from Citadel, with hopes of raising additional funds through the event.

4

Proof of Concept: Integrated Elder & Caregiver CareIn progress

Phase 1 of the Global Grant proposal, "Building a Supportive, Inclusive, and Sustainable Elder Care Ecosystem," is a proof of concept with an estimated budget of ₹60 lakh. It covers an Integrated Health & Social Care Centre, psycho-social support and outreach, and a Caregiver Support Centre with training and community networks. Being developed with Sunidhi Hospital and other partners.

Activities

Regular activities & programs

Ongoing activities that support seniors and the people who care for them.

Caregivers' Circle

Peer support circle for caregivers, facilitated by Prof. Usha Narayanan.

Caring for the Caregiver classes

Classes on self-care, boundaries and time management.

Caregiver support groups

Regular groups to share experiences and reduce isolation.

Grief & loss support groups

A safe space to process grief and ambiguous loss.

Coaching & psychotherapy

Individual support for caregivers' mental health.

Empowering Caregivers lecture series

Talks by healthcare and wellbeing professionals.

Memory cafés

Relaxed social gatherings for people living with memory loss and their carers.

Time-out respite program

Temporary relief for caregivers to prevent burnout.

Music & creative expression

Music sessions, including piano and life-story interviews with residents at Sunandi Elder Care Home.

Plantation & clean-community drives

Community wellness through hygiene and green initiatives with People's Trust and partners.
