Our Work

From community camps to caregiver circles

Here's how EcoSparsh's mission comes to life — through events, outreach, and ongoing support programs for seniors and caregivers alike.

Featured Projects

Our flagship initiatives

1

SeniorCare Community Camp26 January 2025

Organized by EcoSparsh and the Zemyna Foundation, this Senior Care Camp & Fair brought together a holistic blend of health screenings, wellness activities, financial guidance, accessibility support, and engaging experiences tailored for seniors. Senior residents of the community had the opportunity to meet and connect with their neighbors over wholesome food, soulful live music, and brain-stimulation activities. Attendees explored longevity insights inspired by ancient wisdom and visited thoughtfully curated stalls promoting positive ageing.

2

Awareness Program

This project-based awareness program was designed to engage and educate youth on the critical importance of senior care, with a special focus on mental well-being and the role of caregivers. Through interactive experiences, discussions, and community involvement, the initiative built empathy and a sense of responsibility among younger generations toward the emotional and psychological needs of the elderly and the caregivers who support them.

Delivered in partnership with:

  • Rotaract Club of Presidency University
  • People's Trust
  • Sunandi Wellbeing
  • Maarga
  • MindCare
  • Nagarjuna College
  • ...and others, in person
3

Fund Raising Event16 May 2025

EcoSparsh partnered with Citadel to organize a fundraising initiative titled "Junior Charity Golf." Alongside this, EcoSparsh is collaborating with Sunandi Wellbeing to launch a dedicated program supported by an initial grant of ₹1 lakh from Citadel under CSR, with hopes of raising additional funds through the event.
