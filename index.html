What We Do

Care that reaches the whole family

Elder care involves providing support with daily living, companionship, healthcare needs, medication management, emotional support, and specialized care for conditions like dementia and Alzheimer's. EcoSparsh ensures safety, dignity, and an enhanced quality of life for seniors — whether at home or in assisted living — with the help of well-trained and emotionally balanced caregivers.

Mental Wellness

Depression is common among the elderly, who often feel excluded and increasingly isolated once their children have grown and moved on. We work to meet this need directly.

Caregivers

Trained individuals who provide assistance and support to elderly individuals facing physical, emotional, or cognitive challenges due to aging.

Community Wellness

Promoting hygiene and clean environments to foster a sense of unity among neighbors and strengthen community bonds.

Why This Matters

The need is real, and growing

1 in 7 Indians are affected by mental disorders of varying severity
83% of elder care help in India comes from unpaid family caregivers
2/3 of caregivers are women, often carrying this role for 10+ years
42.5% of private sector employees report depression or general anxiety
