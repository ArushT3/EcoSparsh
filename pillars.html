Our Pillars

Three pillars, one connected ecosystem

Everything EcoSparsh does rests on three main pillars, each supporting the others.

PILLAR 01

Elder Care

Dignified, independent and joyful living for seniors, at home and in their community.

  • Routine health screenings
  • Chronic condition management
  • Mobile healthcare units
  • Assisted living & elder day-care centers
  • Social programs to combat loneliness
  • Transportation & home modifications
  • Emergency alert systems
PILLAR 02

Caregivers

Caregivers are the backbone of our healthcare and support systems. Caring for them is caring for seniors.

  • Comprehensive caregiver training
  • Peer support and caregiver circles
  • Legal & financial guidance
  • Background checks & certification
  • Recognition & retention programs
  • Respite care to prevent burnout
  • Telemedicine, mobile app & digital literacy workshops
PILLAR 03

Mental Health & Wellbeing

Reducing stigma and providing real support for seniors and caregivers facing isolation, grief and stress.

  • Access to mental health services
  • Counseling, coaching & psychotherapy
  • Grief and loss support groups
  • Memory cafés and creative expression through music
  • Youth awareness on senior mental wellbeing
  • Online platforms for real-time support
Enablers

What holds the pillars up

Digital Integration

Telemedicine and remote monitoring, a mobile app for elder care management, and AI-driven health tracking and alerts.

Policy & Advocacy

Advocacy for elder-centric policies, care standards and quality regulation, privacy and data rights, and involving elders in decisions that affect them.
